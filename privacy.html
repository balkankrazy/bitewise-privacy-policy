Bitewise Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 18, 2026

1. Introduction

Bitewise respects your privacy. This policy explains how information may be collected, used, and protected.

2. Information We May Collect

  • Account and contact information.
  • Meal planning preferences.
  • Grocery lists and food budget information.
  • Food spending information you enter.
  • App usage and diagnostic information.
  • Subscription and purchase status.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to provide meal planning, grocery lists, budgeting features, subscriptions, customer support, and app improvements.

4. Payments and Subscriptions

Premium subscriptions are processed through the applicable app store. Payment information is handled according to the app store's privacy policies.

5. Information Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate Bitewise or comply with legal obligations.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information, although no electronic system can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained as necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law. Users may contact us to request access to or deletion of their information.

8. Children's Privacy

Bitewise is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Changes will be published on this page.
