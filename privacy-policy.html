Bitewise Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

Bitewise respects your privacy. This policy explains how the current version of the Bitewise app handles information.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

Bitewise is designed to store information locally on your device. This may include meal preferences, dietary settings, meal plans, grocery lists, food budgets, and app preferences. This information helps provide the app's features.

2. Financial Information

The current version of Bitewise does not connect to bank accounts or access banking credentials or transactions. Automatic bank tracking may be considered for a future version. If introduced, this policy will be updated.

3. Personal Information and Permissions

The current version does not require users to create an account or sign in. Its core meal-planning features do not require access to contacts, photos, or location.

4. Advertising

Bitewise does not currently use a confirmed third-party advertising network.

5. Subscriptions and Payments

Bitewise is developing optional Premium subscription features. If subscriptions become available, purchases will be handled through the applicable app-store billing system. The payment provider's privacy practices will also apply.

6. Data Security and Retention

Information stored locally is subject to the security of your device and operating system. You can remove locally stored app information by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app, subject to device backups.

7. Children's Privacy

Bitewise is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as Bitewise adds features or changes how information is handled. The latest version will be made available through the app or its public privacy-policy page.
